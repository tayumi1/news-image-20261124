The World in One Day — Tuesday, 24 November 2026. A single 1024x1024
infographic summing up the day's world news in six verified stories:

1. **Ukraine sinks Russian warship in Libya** — naval drones sank the
   corvette Merkury and damaged the cargo ship Angara at Benghazi.
2. **COP31 collapses in Antalya** — talks ran ~50 hours into overtime,
   then collapsed as 90+ nations rejected a draft with no mention of
   fossil fuels.
3. **US sanctions Iran's shadow banks** — Treasury's "Operation Economic
   Outcast" hit 22+ entities tied to $14.7B in illicit flows.
4. **Israeli strikes kill 3 in Gaza** — victims included UNRWA worker
   Adel al-Helou at the Jabalia camp.
5. **Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang** — the board unanimously brought
   back its CEO after President Trump intervened.
6. **Factory fire in Indonesia** — a major blaze at an industrial
   facility left multiple casualties, workers missing, rescue ongoing.

Built with Python + PIL. Every story was verified against multiple news
sources (AP, Al Jazeera, US Treasury, Grist, Middle East Eye) on the day.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.png` (1024x1024) in the repository root.
